This service is operated from the Republic of Korea, and this policy is written under Korean law — principally the Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA).

"오늘의 너" (the "app") takes your personal information seriously and complies with the Personal Information Protection Act and related Korean law.

1. What we promise up front

This app carries no advertising, never sells or hands your records to anyone else, and contains no analytics or behaviour-tracking tools. It runs on subscription income.

Period, symptom, gynecology and intimacy records are sensitive data (health information) under Article 23 of Korea's Personal Information Protection Act. We ask for your consent to these separately from everything else at sign-up. They are the substance of cycle tracking and prediction, so consent is required — without it the service cannot be used. That said, what your partner sees is entirely yours to decide, item by item, after you sign up. Gynecology records, intimacy records and notes are not shared by default.

2-3. Partner link information

Invite code, link status and time of linking, and your per-item sharing settings.

2-4. Messages between partners

The short phrases you send each other, and when they were sent and read.

2-5. My notes (partner role)

Free-form notes a partner-role user writes for a given date. (For example: "pick up a heat pack", "go to the appointment together next week")

Only you can see them. They are never passed to the other person and are not subject to the sharing settings. They are stored independently of the partner link and remain yours even if you unlink. They are deleted when you delete your account.

2-6. Payment information

Subscription product, store transaction identifier, subscription expiry time, and whether a trial is active.

We do not collect card numbers, bank account numbers or any other payment credentials. Payment is handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play; the app only receives the outcome.

2-7. What we do not collect

  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA and the like), behavioural analytics or tracking data
  • Location, contacts, photos, call or message logs
  • Device unique identifiers

4. You control what your partner sees

What reaches your partner is switched on and off item by item, by you. (Cycle, symptoms, mood, sleep, cravings, gynecology records, intimacy records, notes)

  • Gynecology records, intimacy records and notes are not shared by default.
  • Turning on "hide everything for today" keeps every item out of your partner's view for that period.
  • Items you switch off are never sent from the server to your partner — they are not merely hidden in the app.
  • If you unlink, the other person immediately loses access to everything.
  • A partner can only see records from the day you linked onward. Anything logged before that stays invisible regardless of sharing settings, and stays that way even if you link with the same person again.

5. Sharing with third parties

We do not provide your personal information to third parties. We never sell it, rent it, or use it for advertising, under any circumstances.

The one exception is a lawful request from an investigative authority made through due process, which we must follow.

7. Retention and destruction

  • Personal information is destroyed without delay when you delete your account.
  • You can delete your account yourself from Settings → Delete account in the app. Doing so immediately deletes everything along with the account — period records, symptoms, mood, sleep, notes, messages and my notes. Deleted records cannot be recovered.
  • The partner link is released automatically before deletion, so the other person can no longer see anything.

If you unlink from a partner

  • The moment you unlink, the other person can see nothing.
  • Messages exchanged in that link, and the sharing settings, are destroyed automatically 100 days after unlinking. Neither side can view them during that time, and they are not restored if you link again. (The delay exists only as a minimum grace period in case someone unlinks by mistake and wants it back.)
  • Your own period, symptom and mood records are kept regardless of unlinking, because they belong to you rather than to the couple. A partner's "my notes" are kept for the same reason.
  • Payment and transaction records that Korean law requires us to keep — such as under the Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce — are retained for the required period (five years for records of payment and supply of goods, three years for records of consumer complaints or dispute handling) and then destroyed.

9. Children under 14

The app does not accept sign-ups from children under the age of 14. If we confirm that a user is under 14, we delete that account and its records without delay.

10. How we keep it safe

  • Encryption in transit: all communication between the app and the server is encrypted with TLS.
  • Encryption at rest: the database is encrypted at the storage level.
  • Passwords: stored with one-way encryption; not even the operator can read them.
  • Access control: Row Level Security is applied at the database, so you can only read your own records and the records someone has allowed you to see.
  • Minimal collection: we do not collect information the service does not need.

Note that end-to-end encryption is not applied. Technically, data can be read on the server to the extent required to operate the service. The operator does not read user records except where unavoidable, such as responding to an outage, and never provides them to a third party under any circumstances.
