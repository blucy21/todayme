Frequently asked questions

The things people get stuck on in Today's You. If yours isn't here, write to plumcat@gmail.com. We usually reply within a couple of days.

Getting started

Can I use it on my own?

Yes. Connecting a partner is optional.

Logging, predictions, the calendar and the report all work exactly the same alone. If you change your mind later you can connect then, and everything you've written stays.

How do we connect?

The person who tracks gets a 6-character invite code. Share it with your partner, who enters it in the app.

  • The code is uppercase letters and digits only. Lowercase typing is converted automatically.
  • One app, two people — each signs in with their own account.

It says the invite code is invalid

One of three things:

  • The code was copied wrong — check digit 0 against letter O, and digit 1 against letter I.
  • You're already connected to someone. Disconnect in Settings first, then try again.
  • You entered your own code.

If your connection dropped for a moment when you tapped, you may in fact be connected already. Close the app and open it again to check.

Sharing

How much of my log can my partner see?

You decide item by item, in Settings → Sharing.

  • On to begin with — cycle, symptoms, mood, sleep, cravings
  • Off to begin with — gynecology notes, intimacy, memo

The three sensitive ones are private by default. Turning them on should be the tracker's choice, not something a default decides for them.

One more thing: your partner only sees entries from the day you connected. Anything written before that stays with you.

I want to hide everything, just for today

Settings → Sharing → "Private for today." It covers everything for that day regardless of your item settings, and lifts by itself at midnight.

Your partner's screen catches up within a minute, even if they're sitting in the app.

Can I see the notes my partner writes?

No. A partner's "My notes" are theirs alone. The tracker never sees them.

Predictions

The prediction is off

A prediction is an estimate calculated from what you've logged.

  • With only one cycle logged, we fall back to a general average (28 days) and label it an estimate on screen.
  • We average your most recent six cycles. After two or three, it gets noticeably better.
  • If your cycles vary a lot, we give you a range rather than a single day. We'd rather not pretend to know.

I stopped for a while and now it says "Welcome back"

After more than three months without an entry, predictions stop.

Pregnancy, birth control, or simply forgetting — taking months off is ordinary. But if we kept counting we'd tell you "300 days past your expected date," which judges your body today from a year-old record. It isn't accurate and it isn't kind.

Log the first day of this period and we'll rebuild the prediction.

Can I use this for birth control?

No. This app is not a medical device, and the fertile window is an estimate calculated from your entries. Please talk to a healthcare professional about contraception or planning a pregnancy.

If something feels different from usual, or lasts, trust the clinic over the app.

Notifications

I'm not getting notifications

  • Notifications are for the tracker only. Partner accounts don't have this setting.
  • Check Settings → Notifications in the app.
  • Check iOS Settings → Notifications → Today's You as well. Once you decline, the app can't ask again — you have to turn it on in iOS Settings.

What will I be notified about?

  • 2 days before your period is expected
  • 5 days past the expected date with no start logged
  • Past two weeks, along with a suggestion to see a doctor
  • When a period runs past your usual length with no end logged

If seeing the details on your lock screen feels like too much, you can hide the wording in Settings. The notification still arrives; you open the app to see what it's about.

You say there are no ads. Really?

Yes. And something that matters more:

  • No ads
  • Your records are never sold or handed to anyone
  • Zero analytics or tracking tools in the app

The details are in the Privacy Policy.

I got a new phone

Sign in with the same email and everything follows you. Your records live on the server; the app is a window onto them.

We broke up. I want to disconnect

Settings → Sharing → Disconnect.

  • They lose access to your records immediately.
  • Your records stay. Keep using it alone, or connect with someone new later.
  • Notes your partner wrote for themselves stay with them.

I want to delete my account and everything in it

In the app: Settings → Delete account. Your account and every record are deleted immediately, and it cannot be undone.

No email to us, no waiting.
